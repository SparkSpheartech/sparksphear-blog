## How to use this workbook

Complete one section at a time. Start with one workflow that happens often, has a clear owner, and creates measurable friction. Do not automate a process you cannot describe or verify - if you cannot write down the steps, the workbook will not fix that, and neither will software.

## Free preview: Section 1 - Workflow inventory

List the recurring workflows that consume time or create avoidable errors. For each one, capture:

- **The trigger:** what starts the workflow (a call, a booking, an invoice date)
- **The steps:** who touches it, in what order, in which tools
- **The handoffs:** where information is copied, re-typed, or re-entered
- **The failure mode:** what goes wrong when it is done manually (missed call, late invoice, double booking)
- **The frequency and cost:** how often it happens, and roughly what an hour of it costs

Fill this in for one real workflow this week. Owners who complete just this section usually find one or two workflows worth more than the price of the full Kit - which is the point.
